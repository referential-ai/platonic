# AU3 Public Audio Fixtures

These WAV files are non-human, purpose-built test fixtures. No microphone or
personal audio was used. Referential AI releases the fixture recordings under
CC0-1.0.

- `spoken-question.wav` was synthesized from the authored text "What is the
  capital of France?" with eSpeak NG 1.52.0 (`en-us`, 150 words/minute), then
  converted to 16 kHz mono signed 16-bit PCM and padded with 500 ms of leading
  and trailing silence by FFmpeg n8.1.2.
- `steady-noise.wav` was generated by FFmpeg n8.1.2 `anoisesrc` using white
  noise, amplitude `0.003`, seed `328`, and a three-second duration. It is a
  deterministic below-threshold no-hallucination fixture.

`manifest.json` records the exact PCM properties, expected text, and SHA-256
checksums. Live device proof must never replace or modify these tracked files.
