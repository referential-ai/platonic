Why Platonic

A common foundation, made concrete.

Platonic is the server: it owns workspaces, agents, threads, tools, policy, approvals, and ledgers. Plato Agent is the client distribution that makes those boundaries visible.

Diligence by design

Visible by construction.

The ledger is the run. Interfaces, transcripts, recovery, and audits are derived from recorded facts rather than hidden control flow.
